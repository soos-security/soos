**Not challenged.** The unlock call carries the candidate's own `SessionId`, and step 13 re-reads that ID fresh and requires it to be locked, bound, of the candidate UID and of the step-7 `Name`. Making a newcomer appear needs either the credentials of its owner or another user's own login, neither of which gains access to the candidate's session. No concrete fail-open exists.
