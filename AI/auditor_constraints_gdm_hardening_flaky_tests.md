### Pre-existing violations found (not introduced by this change)

- `pam_stack.rs::read_bounded_utf8` (include targets, followed like libpam) opens without `O_NOCTTY`, after which the
  type check runs on the descriptor: a target symlinked to a tty device could become the controlling terminal of a
  session-less root process, and opening a device node may have open-time side effects. Root-owned `/etc/pam.d`
  only; adding `O_NOCTTY` there is harmless and recommended (optional, not required by the spec or tests).
- `gdm.rs::get_gdm_status` checks `pam_file.is_file()` by path before reading (read-only, the read itself refuses
  non-regular files on the descriptor): no impact, out of scope (§4.2 last paragraph).
